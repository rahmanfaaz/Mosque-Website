About Us

At Jamia Masjid West Drayton, we are a dedicated team of individuals committed to serving our community with compassion and integrity. Our aim is to provide a welcoming environment for worship and community activities, fostering a spirit of unity and support among all members.

We believe in the importance of strong leadership and community service, which is reflected in our team of active directors and dedicated members. Together, we strive to uphold the values of Islam and contribute positively to our local community.
